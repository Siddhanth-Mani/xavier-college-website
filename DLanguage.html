Overview & Philosophy

About the Language Department

The Languages department plays an important role in Xavier college as it functions to impart knowledge which enables students to express themselves adequately in English, a second language to our children and a compulsory examination subject.

English is important for students as it broadens their minds, develops emotional skills, improves the quality of life by providing job opportunities. Moreover, the use of English as an International language is growing with time because it is the only medium for communication in many countries.

It is the language of science, aviation, computers, diplomacy and tourism. Last but not the least, it is the language of international communication, the media and the internet. Whether it is for professional or personal reasons, understanding the importance of English will help you reach your goals.

Knowledge of English equips you with the tools to travel and communicate more effectively in so many parts of the world, which, in turn, allows to you to explore and travel more confidently and to learn more about the countries and cultures you visit.

Global language means the language which is spoken internationally in the world. The language which people can easily understand and speak around the world.

The English language was spread from the 1600s to the 1900s by the British people. British people were the first people to play a very important role in spreading the English language.

The English language becomes the most common, useful, and native language of most countries. In many cases, the English language helps people in different aspects.

In the 20th century, America decided the English language as the global or international language of the world.

Most of the books and comics are published in the English language.

All people can easily understand and read it.

As the English language has became the most popular and common language among the people.

Most movies and television dramas are made in the English language and watched around the world.

The English language is the language of the internet. Most of the content available on the internet is in the English language and mostly non-native English speakers access it around the world.
