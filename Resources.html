Digital Pedagogy

Digital Learning at Xavier College

The effective use of digital learning tools in classrooms enhances student engagement, supports teachers in personalizing lesson plans, and equips young learners with critical 21st-century digital competencies.

Web-based learning provides structured online course content, supplementary revision materials, and academic research access. Xavier College encourages all students to utilize these vetted national and international learning portals.
