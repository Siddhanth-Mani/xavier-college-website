Overview & Philosophy

About the Social Science & Geography Department

Social science prepares students to live peacefully in a community. Our community functions with norms, beliefs, culture, tradition and laws. Therefore it is important to teach and deal with problems that are acceptable to the society and community as a whole.

By providing relevant information and knowledge, skills and attitudes, the study of Social Science prepares students to grow up as active, responsible, and reflective members of society. It also teaches them to address societal and global concerns using literature, technology and other identifiable community resources.

The social sciences are important because they help people understand how to not only analyze their own behavior, but also the behavior and motivations of their peers. The social sciences also give us a better understanding of how to create more inclusive, and effective institutions.

Curriculum Scope

Key Subjects & Topics

  • Fijian & Pacific History: Pre-colonial to Modern Governance
  • World History: Global Revolutions & International Relations
  • Physical Geography: Geomorphology, Weather & Climate Systems
  • Human Geography: Population, Urbanization & Development
  • Topographic Map Reading, GIS & Geographic Fieldwork
  • Civic Studies: Democratic Governance, Rights & Community Law
